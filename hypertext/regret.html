What if I regret it?

You start imagining everyone having fun without you.

You picture the pictures that might get posted later.

You already feel like you missed something, even though the night hasn't even happened yet.

Then you think about the other possibility.

What if you go and wish you stayed home?

It feels like you are going to regret one choice no matter what you do.

Maybe I should just go.

Maybe staying home is okay.
